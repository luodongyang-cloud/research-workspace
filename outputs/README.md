Concept schematic of zero-bias photosensing and voltage-weighted readout in a GaON/GaN photomemristive 2TG1C1S pixel array. Optical history establishes conductance G; capacitor voltage V_C stores the electrical weight. Pixelwise products are summed into one scalar per weight template. The four response traces share a time axis and are illustrative, not experimental measurements.

Zero externally applied bias applies to sensing; weighted readout applies the capacitor voltage to the sensor. Short approximately ohmic readout and small capacitor droop are assumed. Author scientific review is required.
